# US SEO launch and off-page plan

The site is technically configured for US English and USD-first ecommerce intent. SEO cannot
guarantee traffic from one country or block organic visitors from another. Measure the US share
in GA4 and Search Console; use ad-platform geographic controls for paid acquisition.

## One-time launch actions

1. Add the production domain as a Google Search Console **Domain property** and verify it by DNS.
2. Submit `https://<production-domain>/sitemap.xml` in Search Console.
3. Inspect the homepage, `/tools`, and the five highest-value calculator URLs. Request indexing
   only after the production canonical is confirmed on each page.
4. Add the domain to Bing Webmaster Tools and import the Search Console property.
5. Connect GA4, then create a comparison for `Country exactly matches United States` and track
   organic calculator use, saved scenarios, and result copies.
6. Validate one calculator in Google's Rich Results Test. The remaining calculators use the same
   shared schema builder.

## US-first content program

Publish useful, original material rather than changing a year in generic posts. Recommended
clusters:

- US payment processing fee comparison with sourced, dated fee tables.
- Shopify profit benchmarks by US product category using a clearly explained methodology.
- State sales-tax calculation workflow, explicitly distinguishing calculation from tax advice.
- Amazon, Etsy, Walmart Marketplace, and TikTok Shop fee explainers for US sellers.
- US shipping-cost and free-shipping-threshold worksheets using current carrier data.
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday margin planning and break-even ROAS tools.

Each article should link to the calculator that performs the calculation, cite primary sources,
show an original worked example, name an author/reviewer, and carry a real reviewed date.

## Off-page acquisition

Do not buy links, exchange links at scale, or publish templated guest posts. Build links to assets
that are useful enough to cite:

- Pitch ecommerce newsletters and US agency resource pages with one calculator or original data
  asset that directly fits an existing article.
- Offer calculators as free resources to Shopify developers, ecommerce educators, and university
  entrepreneurship programs.
- Publish product profiles only on relevant software directories, with consistent brand copy and
  the canonical homepage URL.
- Turn original benchmark studies into a downloadable CSV and methodology page so journalists
  can verify and cite the figures.
- Reclaim unlinked brand mentions and fix broken links to EcomNivo before pursuing new placements.

Suggested outreach format:

> Subject: Free calculator for your [topic] resource
>
> I noticed your guide explains [specific concept]. EcomNivo's free [calculator] lets a reader
> run that exact calculation in USD and shows the formula and worked example. If it improves the
> resource, you can find it here: [canonical URL]. No account or affiliate arrangement is needed.

## Monthly quality checks

- Search Console: US clicks, impressions, query relevance, index coverage, and Core Web Vitals.
- GA4: US organic sessions, calculator completion, saved scenarios, result copies, and revenue/RPM.
- Crawl: 200 status, self-canonical, unique title/H1/description, `en-US`, schema validity, and one
  or more internal links to every indexable page.
- Links: new referring domains, lost links, anchor concentration, and spammy placements to avoid.
- Content: refresh only when fees, platform rules, tax guidance, or source data genuinely changes.
